Daily Board – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 21 September 2026

Daily Board ("the app") is a calm daily planner with a yearly "Vision & Goals" page and a habit "Streaks" tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your tasks, goals and habits stay on your device. We do not run a server for them and we do not require an account.

1. Who is responsible
Sakir Yenilmez, Gotenstr. 8, 54293 Trier, Germany
E-mail: yenilmez.apps@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device
Everything you enter — tasks, yearly goals, your one-line vision sentence, habits and their check-in history, and your settings (language, theme, reminder times) — is stored locally on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data. Note that your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account) may include app data according to your device settings; we do not control that.

3. Notifications and widget
Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device; no push-notification server is involved. The app may ask for the notification permission and for an exemption from battery optimization so reminders arrive on time. The home-screen widget displays your local tasks on your device only.

4. Purchases (Premium)
Optional Premium features are sold through Google Play. To manage purchases and restore them across devices, the app uses the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). When you view or purchase Premium, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and technical information such as device type, OS version and country. It does not receive your tasks, goals or habits. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

5. Advertising
The app currently shows no ads and does not initialize any advertising service. If banner ads (Google AdMob) are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before they go live, and the required consent choices will be offered where applicable.

6. What we do not do
We do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. We do not use analytics or crash-tracking services, we do not sell data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

7. Feedback and sharing
If you send feedback from within the app, it opens your own e-mail app with a pre-filled message to us; we receive only what you choose to send. The "Share app" and "Rate us" actions open the standard Android share sheet or the Google Play page.

8. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you on our own servers, there is generally nothing to access, correct or delete on our side — you control your data directly in the app and by uninstalling it. If you are in the EU/EEA you additionally have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the e-mail above for any request about data processed via RevenueCat or Google Play in connection with your purchases.

10. Changes
We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if ads are added). The date at the top shows the latest version.
