Privacy Policy
US Citizenship Test (Android app) · Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information the "US Citizenship Test" app (the "App") handles and why. The App is an independent study tool for the U.S. naturalization civics test. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by USCIS, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, or any U.S. government agency.

1. No account, no personal profile
The App does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.

2. Data stored on your device
Your study progress (which questions you have answered, bookmarks, streak, mock test results), your settings (language, theme, reminder time), and your chosen state are stored only on your device. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Microphone and Speak Practice
Speak Practice uses your device's built-in speech recognition service, which requires microphone permission. Your speech is processed by that service (on Android, typically provided by Google) under its own privacy policy. The App compares the recognized text to the official answers on your device, does not record or store your audio, does not keep your spoken transcripts, and does not send them to us. You can decline the microphone permission and keep using all other features.

4. Analytics
We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how features are used. Events contain only non-personal metadata such as a question number, a question category, a feature name, or a mock test result. We never log speech, audio, or personal information. Firebase may automatically collect standard technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, and a randomly generated app-instance identifier.

5. Advertising
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. AdMob may still collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID) and technical data to deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings. Purchasing Premium removes ads.

6. In-app purchases
Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see or store your payment information. The App only receives confirmation that a purchase was made so it can unlock Premium.

7. Notifications
The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It does not involve any server or personal data.

8. Children
The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-party services
The App uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
